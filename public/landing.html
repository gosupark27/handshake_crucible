Your journey: promise, private action, shared outcome.

Give your word

Pick what you intend to do. Everyone can see your promise.

Make your choice

Commit one action. You can follow your promise or choose differently. Your action stays sealed until resolution.

See what changed

When everyone commits or time expires, actions resolve together. The ledger records accepted events, and the next crossing begins.

Reach peace when four different travelers each give freely, before nine crossings pass or pressure reaches nine.

Four choices. A shared consequence.

Give freely

Give up two supplies. Each traveler can make this sacrifice once. Four different givers bring peace.

Send supplies

Send one supply to another traveler. Choose a recipient. Transfers are closed during the second crossing.

Stay silent

Keep your supplies and wait. Your choice still counts when the group resolves the crossing.

Hold close

Gain one supply for yourself. Pressure rises by one, bringing the group closer to fracture.

Each crossing introduces a new situation. Read the scene before making your promise.
